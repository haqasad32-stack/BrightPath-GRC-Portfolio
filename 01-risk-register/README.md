# Cybersecurity Risk Register

This section contains my cybersecurity risk register for the fictional BrightPath Retail Group case study.

The register documents five cybersecurity risks, including their potential business impact, likelihood and impact scores, existing controls, control gaps, risk owners, recommended treatments, estimated residual risk, and relevant NIST Cybersecurity Framework mappings.

**Important:** BrightPath Retail Group is fictional. Risk scenarios and assessment results are simulated for educational and portfolio purposes. Residual risk ratings are estimates, not verified outcomes.
