# Cybersecurity Risk Register

## Project Overview

This risk register is part of my Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) portfolio. I created a fictional retail company, BrightPath Retail Group, to practice identifying cybersecurity risks, assessing their potential business impact, and recommending controls to reduce exposure.

The register contains five cybersecurity risks covering employee devices, Microsoft 365 accounts, customer information, Third party vendors, and backup recovery. I documented each risk and evaluated its likelihood and impact to help prioritize remediation.

## What I Assessed

The five risks included in this assessment are:

1. **Endpoint Malware:** The risk of malware infecting employee laptops because of weaknesses in endpoint security.
2. **Microsoft 365 Account Compromise:** The risk of unauthorized account access when multi-factor authentication is not consistently enforced.
3. **Excessive Database Permissions:** The risk of employees having more access to customer information than their jobs require.
4. **Third-Party Vendor Risk:** The risk of a vendor's security weaknesses exposing BrightPath's information.
5. **Backup and Recovery:** The risk of business data not being recoverable when needed because backup restoration has not been regularly tested.

## Assessment Approach

For each risk, I documented the affected asset, threat, vulnerability, risk statement, existing controls, control gap, risk owner, and recommended treatment.

I assigned likelihood and impact ratings using a five-point scale and calculated the inherent risk score by multiplying the two ratings. I also included a projected residual risk score to estimate the remaining risk if the recommended controls are implemented effectively.

The recommendations focus on practical improvements, including stronger endpoint protection, multi-factor authentication, least-privilege access, vendor security reviews, and regular backup restoration testing.

## Risk Scoring

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

| Score | Rating   |
| ----- | -------- |
| 1–5   | Low      |
| 6–10  | Medium   |
| 11–15 | High     |
| 16–25 | Critical |

Residual risk ratings are estimates, not verified outcomes. The recommended controls have not been validated in a real company environment.

## Framework Mapping

I mapped each risk to relevant outcomes in the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0**. This helped me connect the risks and recommended actions to recognized cybersecurity practices.

These mappings are references for organizing the assessment and do not represent a claim of NIST compliance.

## Scope and Limitations

BrightPath Retail Group is a fictional organization created for educational and portfolio purposes. The scenarios, company details, risk scores, control descriptions, and assessment results are hypothetical. This project does not use or evaluate confidential information from Walmart or any other real organization.

This is a portfolio exercise, not a formal audit or a real-world security assessment.
